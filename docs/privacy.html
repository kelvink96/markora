Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Markora is built to work on your device. Here is exactly what it stores and when it touches the network.

What we collect

Nothing. Markora has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. We do not receive your documents, your settings or anything about how you use the app.

What is stored on your device

Everything Markora remembers stays on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Settings: theme, colour scheme, editor options and your new-document template. On desktop they are saved in the app data folder (for example %APPDATA%\com.markora.app\ on Windows). On the web they are saved in your browser's local storage.
  • Session: your open tabs and recent files, so Markora can reopen them next time. You can turn session restore off in Settings.
  • Web workspaces: in the web app, documents you work on in the browser are kept in your browser's IndexedDB storage. Clearing your site data removes them.
  • Your files: Markdown files are saved wherever you choose. Markora only reads and writes files you pick through the file dialog.

When you copy or paste, Markora uses your system clipboard. That content stays on your device.

When Markora uses the network

  • Update check (desktop): when Check automatically is on in Settings, or when you choose Check for updates, the app fetches a small file, kelvink96.github.io/markora/latest.json, from GitHub Pages. If a newer version exists it downloads the installer from GitHub. No personal information is sent, though GitHub sees your IP address as any web host does. Turn the setting off and the app does not check on its own.
  • Fonts: the app and this website load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may log those requests under its own privacy policy.
  • Links: links you click in a document open in your default browser, and that site's own policy applies.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To show the latest version and release notes, the home page asks the GitHub API for the latest release. Downloading the installer from GitHub Releases is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. For fonts, see Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The history is in the public repository.

Contact
